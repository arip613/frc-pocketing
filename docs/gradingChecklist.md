# Grading checklist for rating sheets

Claude grades marathon rating sheets against this list. It combines the user's own
feedback (first) with the rules in `docs/pocketingResearch.md`. The user's grades always
override Claude's on the same design.

## User's stated preferences

These come from comparison feedback on 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30:

- No solid blobs. Any area where a pocket fits should be pocketed, including the strips
  along walls and cutouts.
- Ribs from hole to hole, so that neighbouring holes are linked directly.
- Symmetric wherever the plate is. X-bracing is welcome in big open cells.
- Not too sparse. The user preferred the denser designs when the two were close.
- Pockets reach the wall and cutouts instead of stopping short of them.

## Research rules, used as tie-breakers

- Ribs run straight between holes, along load paths, not off into nowhere.
- No hole hangs off a single rib.
- Rib widths are even. Corners are rounded, with no wedges or slivers.
- Fewer, well-sized pockets beat many crumbs. Cells are triangle-ish, not slots.

## Grades

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Would sit next to the example plates: every preference met, with nothing to fix |
| 4 | Good, with one small flaw (one small blob, one missing link, slight asymmetry) |
| 3 | Acceptable but generic, or two or three small flaws |
| 2 | Clearly off: a big blob, a starburst, slivers, crumbs, one huge pocket, or much too sparse |
| 1 | Ugly or broken-looking |

Half grades are allowed. Held-out plates are never graded.
