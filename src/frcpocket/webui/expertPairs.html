Plate Pairs

Each screen shows two pocketing designs for the same plate. Pick the one you'd rather cut, the way you would in a design review. These pairs are deliberately close calls, which is where your eye helps most.

  • Click the better one, or press ← for the left one and → for the right one.
  • If you really can't separate them, press ↓ (about the same). Use it sparingly.
  • Backspace undoes. Your progress is saved in this browser.
  • You can stop and hit Export picks at any point. Send the file back.
