Plate Rater · part 7

You'll see FRC plates with lightening pockets, one at a time. Some were designed by teams and vendors, some by a pocketing tool we're training. Rate each one on how good the pocketing is, the way you would if a teammate showed it to you in a design review.

  • 1 means you'd send it back. 9 means you'd cut it as is.
  • Judge the pocketing, not the plate outline or the hole layout. Those are fixed.
  • Go with your gut. A couple of seconds per plate is plenty.
  • Keys: 1–9 to rate, S to skip, Backspace to undo.
  • O (or the button above the plate) shows the plate with no pockets next to the team's own pocketed version, where there is one.
  • D lets you fix the design by hand: Add rib (drag a straight line), Eraser (paint over material to cut it away, such as a rib the tool shouldn't have made; [ and ] change its size), or the Marker to circle a problem. Drawings are saved with your ratings and come back in the export.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser. You can stop and export at any point, even after 50.
